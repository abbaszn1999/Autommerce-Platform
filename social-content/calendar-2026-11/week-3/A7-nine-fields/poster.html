ChatGPT Shopping

Nine fields
between your product
and ChatGPT.

OpenAI rejects the whole row if any one of them is empty.
01item_idA stable ID, never reused
02titleThe exact product, variant included
03descriptionThe facts, in plain text
04urlThe page for this variant
05brandThe brand, as printed
06seller_nameWho is selling it
07image_urlA photo of this variant
08availabilityIn stock, or say it isn't
09priceThe price, with its currency
9

Miss one, and the product never enters
the conversation.
