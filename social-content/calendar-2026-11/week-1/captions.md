- Visuals: margins, alignment, no overlaps or clipped text, no widows, legible at phone size. Platform screens are cropped to hide balances and account details.
- Text: no em dashes, no AI filler phrases, every third-party number has a source, no unverified performance claims.
- Claims: the Persona size feature is described as a "size suggestion". The partner post shows no terms until they're confirmed.
