# Autommerce social content: quality standards

These rules apply to every post for the four channels (Abbas Zein LinkedIn, Autommerce LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, including the Persona lane). Nothing goes to Publer until it passes every check below.

## 1. Screen recordings, demos and animations

The goal is a demo that looks like a skilled product marketer recorded it by hand.

- **Pick the right tool for the topic.** Every recording shows the exact feature the post talks about, doing the job the caption describes. No generic dashboard footage.
- **Plan the take before recording.** Write the click path, the prompt or input data, and the moment of value (the "after"). Rehearse once, then record.
- **Use clean, believable sample data.** Realistic product names, prices and images. No test strings, no lorem ipsum, no other merchant's real data, no personal data.
- **Clean frame.** Hide balances, emails, notifications, cookie banners, dev tools and unrelated tabs. Crop out the app header when it shows account details.
- **Motion with purpose.** Zoom in on the element being used, hold long enough to read (at least 1 second per line of text), zoom out to show context. Eased camera moves only, no jumps or shake.
- **Cursor and clicks.** Cursor moves smoothly to the target, click feedback is subtle, no wandering.
- **No overlay bugs.** Text and chips never cover the UI being demonstrated, never overlap each other, never get clipped by the frame or the phone mockup. Check z-order and safe margins.
- **Honest timing.** Compress waiting, never fake results. If a number is shown (seconds, rows), it comes from the actual run.
- **Check every frame.** Review a contact sheet of frames at 1-second intervals plus every transition before export.

## 2. Design (posters, carousels, covers)

Act as Autommerce's senior graphic designer.

- **Brand system:**
  - Colors: orange #F76D01, red #C40000, purple #400095, violet #6B358D, ink #0B0A10, paper #F8F6F3.
  - Signature gradient: orange to red to purple.
  - Type: Geist and Geist Mono (Uni Neue when licensed).
  - Logo: the "A" mark.
  - Canvases: ink for people and fashion, paper for product and data.
- **One idea per visual.** One headline, one key phrase in orange, supporting text only when it adds meaning.
- **Layout:**
  - 72px safe margins on 1080x1350.
  - Consistent baseline and left alignment.
  - Nothing within 40px of an edge except full-bleed imagery.
  - Text never on a busy area without a scrim.
- **Typography:**
  - No widows or orphans.
  - No lines broken mid-phrase.
  - Headlines checked at mobile size (360px wide preview).
- **Imagery:**
  - Abbas visuals: identity anchored on the character sheet, natural expression, real-photo realism.
  - Product visuals: real screenshots or recordings.
  - Generated imagery is never presented as a real customer, store or result.
- **Carousels:**
  - The cover earns the swipe.
  - Each slide carries one point.
  - Progress indicator on every slide.
  - The last slide has the CTA.
- **Export:**
  - Feed PNGs at 2160x2700.
  - LinkedIn PDF at 1080x1350.
  - Video at 1080x1350, 30fps, H.264.

## 3. Copy and marketing

Act as a senior B2B marketer writing for decision makers: CEOs, ecommerce directors, heads of digital and agency owners in MENA and the USA.

- **Lead with the reader's pain.** The first line must stop a busy executive. Then the cost, then how it gets solved, then one clear call to action.
- **Write like a person.**
  - No AI filler: "in today's fast-paced world", "game-changer", "unlock", "revolutionize", "seamless", "leverage", "dive in", "elevate".
  - No stacked emojis.
  - Avoid em dashes.
- **Specific beats impressive.** Real steps, real numbers from real runs, named sources for every third-party statistic.
- **Engagement by design.** End opinion and education posts with a question a decision maker can answer from experience.
- **Calls to action:**
  - Autommerce: `autommerce.com/contact` (Client or Partner).
  - Persona: `persona.autommerce.com/contact`.
  - Personal posts: soft CTA or DM.
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 4 on LinkedIn and 6 to 8 on Instagram. Never inside sentences.
- **Language:** English first. Proofread for grammar, spelling, punctuation, product names and capitalization (Autommerce, Persona, White Friday, Cyber Monday).

## 4. Claims discipline

- "100% accuracy" is an objective. Public wording: "built for exact-product accuracy".
- No ranking, conversion, returns or savings promises without measured proof.
- Third-party numbers carry a source line.
- Illustrative math is labeled "Illustrative example".
- Only features that are live and verified in the product appear in posts.

## 5. Review gate before Publer

Every post is checked twice, once for the visual and once for the text:

1. **Visual:** alignment, spacing, overlaps, clipping, crop, legibility on mobile, brand colors, logo, frame-by-frame for video.
2. **Text:** grammar, facts, claims, links, hashtags, CTA, character limits per network.
3. **Channel fit:**
   - Instagram: media required, 4:5, hook in the first line.
   - LinkedIn: hook in the first 2 lines before "see more".
   - Facebook: shorter, conversational.
4. **Publer:** create drafts first, Abbas approves, then schedule.
