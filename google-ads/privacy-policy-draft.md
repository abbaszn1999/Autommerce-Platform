# Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Autommerce collects through autommerce.com and the Autommerce platform (platform.autommerce.com), why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

### Website visitors
When you use our contact or booking form we collect the details you enter: your name, email address, company, website, number of SKUs, product interest, and any message you write.

When you use the free catalog scan or other tools, we process the website address or catalog data you submit so we can return the result. Our servers may also keep standard technical logs such as IP address and request time to keep the service secure.

### Platform users
When you create an Autommerce account we collect your name, email address, and workspace details. We store the catalog data, files, and content you add to your workspace so we can provide the service. Payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.

### Google Ads and Google Merchant Center data
If you choose to connect a Google Ads account or a Google Merchant Center account to your workspace, you sign in with Google and grant Autommerce access. We request only these permissions:

- **Google Ads** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords`): to read your campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, assets, budgets, and performance reports, and to make changes you request.
- **Google Merchant Center** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/content`): to read your products, product status and issues, and account settings, and to make changes you request.

We access only the accounts you select, and only on behalf of the workspace you connected them to.

## How we use it

We use your details to reply to your request, schedule and hold the meeting you book, assess whether Autommerce is a good fit, provide and support the platform, and send you information you asked for.

We use Google Ads and Merchant Center data only to provide features you use inside Autommerce:

- show your account, campaign, and product data in your workspace;
- let the Autommerce assistant answer your questions and prepare suggestions;
- apply changes to your Google Ads or Merchant Center account **only after a member of your workspace reviews and approves them** (or under automation rules your workspace has turned on), and keep a history of those changes so they can be reviewed and undone.

We do not sell your personal information or your Google data. We do not use Google data for advertising, to build user profiles, or to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. We do not let people read your Google data, except with your permission, when needed for security or to investigate abuse, or when the law requires it.

**Autommerce's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.**

## Scheduling and Google Calendar
Meetings are scheduled through a Google Calendar account owned by Autommerce. When you book a time, we create a calendar event containing your name, email, and the details you submitted, and Google sends you an invitation that may include a Google Meet link. For booking, our use of Google user data is limited to creating and managing these events and checking availability on our own calendar. Booking a meeting does not give us access to your Google account or calendar.

## Who we share it with
We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and the platform, and only as needed to provide the service:

- hosting and database providers;
- email delivery and Google Workspace/Calendar;
- payment processing;
- **Composio**, which securely stores the access granted when you connect Google Ads or Merchant Center and sends requests to Google on our behalf;
- AI model providers that process your request to generate the assistant's answers. We use them with zero data retention and privacy settings turned on, so they do not use this data to train their models.

We also share information when the law requires it. These providers may process data only to perform their services for us. We do not transfer Google data to anyone else except as needed to provide the service, for security, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.

## Security
Access to Google accounts is stored encrypted and is never shown to the assistant or to other workspaces. Each workspace can access only its own connected accounts. Data is encrypted in transit.

## Retention and your rights
We keep enquiry and booking records for as long as needed to handle your request and our business relationship, then delete or anonymise them.

You can disconnect Google Ads or Merchant Center at any time from your workspace settings, or remove Autommerce at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). When you disconnect, we revoke the access right away and delete the cached Google data for that connection within 30 days. We keep the record of changes made through Autommerce while your workspace exists, so your team can audit them, and delete it when the workspace is deleted.

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing info@autommerce.com.

## Changes and contact
We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Email info@autommerce.com.
